Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Original output is preserved as `resources/logo-v2.png`, with a copy beside the HTML prototype at `design/logo-v2.png`.

Prompt:

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: minimal production desktop utility brand mark for Claude Switcher. Design a single precise flat vector-style symbol in muted burnt terracotta (#C77553), isolated on a truly transparent background. Symbol is two opposing horizontal rounded arrows interlocking into one compact geometric monogram, expressing switching between two profiles. Broad confident geometry, beautifully balanced negative space, softened corners, visually distinctive silhouette, legible at 20 pixels. Quiet editorial software branding, warm and human, appropriate beside clean black sans-serif typography on white. Front view, flat solid color, no gradients, no 3D glass, no shadows, no enclosing app tile, no letters, no typography, no starburst, no Anthropic logo, no watermark. Center mark occupies 72% of square with generous transparent margin.

The new mark is wired into the standalone prototype. Integration into the Electron app remains pending design approval.
